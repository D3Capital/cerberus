# Three heads. One journey through time.

CERBERUS is a personal journal built around three perspectives on time.

<div class="about-times">

<div class="about-time about-past">

### Past

**What remains.**

Memory, history and experience.

The past is not simply what has disappeared. It is what survives — in institutions, markets, people, decisions and memory.

</div>

<div class="about-time about-present">

### Present

**What is.**

Observation, decision and life.

The present is where history becomes experience and uncertainty becomes choice.

</div>

<div class="about-time about-future">

### Future

**What may come.**

Possibility, change and becoming.

The future is not something to predict with certainty. It is a field of possibilities shaped by what came before and what we choose now.

</div>

</div>

---

CERBERUS is not dedicated to a single subject.

Markets, history, technology, books, places, people and ordinary life may all appear here.

They are connected by one question:

**How does the past shape the present — and what might the present become?**
